## X Sweet

Soft, warm realism with natural skin tones and a gentle, inviting aesthetic. The most versatile starting point for most prompts.

**Pick this when:**
- You want realistic output with a warm, flattering look
- You're not sure which model to use — X Sweet handles most prompts well
- Skin tone accuracy and natural lighting matter

**Avoid when:** you want dramatic contrast or a stylized look — use X Desire or X Vision instead.

**Example prompt:** `a woman in her 20s, auburn hair, soft sunlight, white summer dress, outdoor cafe, natural light, photorealistic`

---

## Photorealism

Sharp, high-detail photographic output with accurate skin texture and true-to-life proportions.

**Pick this when:**
- You want the result to look like a photograph
- Skin texture, hair detail, and lighting accuracy are the priority
- You want photographic quality as a first frame before animating

**Avoid when:** you want a stylized or illustrated look — use Anime, Cartoon, or 2.5D instead.

**Example prompt:** `a woman in her 30s, dark hair, white linen shirt, sitting by a window, soft natural light, shallow depth of field, photorealistic`

---

## Semi-Realism

A blend of photographic and illustrated — photographic detail with slightly stylized faces and softer lighting.

**Pick this when:**
- You want something between a photo and an illustration
- Stylized facial features with realistic body detail
- You want a polished, slightly idealized look

**Avoid when:** you want strict photorealism — use Photorealism instead.

**Example prompt:** `a woman with long black hair, soft studio lighting, stylized features, detailed skin, semi-realistic portrait`

---

## 2.5D

A rendered look between 3D and flat illustration — clean lines, vibrant colors, and a game-art aesthetic.

**Pick this when:**
- You want a polished rendered look without full 3D weight
- Vibrant colors and clean linework are part of the aesthetic
- You're generating stylized characters with three-dimensional presence

**Avoid when:** you want flat 2D illustration — use Anime or Cartoon instead.

**Example prompt:** `2.5D rendered woman, short blonde hair, futuristic outfit, studio lighting, smooth skin, vibrant colors`

---

## Anime

Clean 2D illustration with bold linework, flat shading, and expressive anime character design.

**Pick this when:**
- You want anime or manga-style output
- Stylized proportions and expressive faces are the goal
- You want a recognizable illustrated aesthetic

**Avoid when:** you want photorealism — use Photorealism or X Sweet instead.

**Example prompt:** `anime girl, long silver hair, school uniform, cherry blossom background, detailed linework, soft shading`

---

## Cartoon

Flat graphic style suited to pin-up, comic, and retro illustration aesthetics.

**Pick this when:**
- You want flat, graphic, or retro illustrative output
- Bold outlines and simplified shading are part of the look
- You're going for a pin-up or comic-book aesthetic

**Avoid when:** you want detailed anime-style linework — use Anime instead.

**Example prompt:** `retro pin-up illustration, woman in a swimsuit, bold outlines, flat colors, 1950s style, graphic poster aesthetic`

---

## Artoon

Detailed illustrative style with a painterly, comic-art feel — more textured and editorial than Cartoon.

**Pick this when:**
- You want hand-drawn illustration quality with rich detail
- A painterly or editorial comic aesthetic is the goal
- You want more texture and depth than flat Cartoon output

**Avoid when:** you want clean flat illustration — use Cartoon instead.

**Example prompt:** `comic-art illustration, woman with tattoos, urban background, detailed linework, painterly shading, editorial style`

---

## X Desire

Cinematic realism with dramatic lighting, deep shadows, and high-contrast editorial output.

**Pick this when:**
- You want a moody, high-production photographic look
- Strong directional light and shadow are part of the composition
- You're generating scenes with a fashion or editorial feel

**Avoid when:** you want soft natural light — use X Sweet or Photorealism instead.

**Example prompt:** `a woman in a red dress, standing in a dark corridor, single overhead light, cinematic color grade, high contrast`

---

## X Vision

Vivid, high-saturation output with a stylized editorial aesthetic and bold color treatment.

**Pick this when:**
- You want vibrant, punchy colors with a stylized look
- A fashion-forward or editorial feel is the goal
- You want something between realism and illustration

**Avoid when:** you want muted, natural tones — use Photorealism instead.

**Example prompt:** `editorial portrait, woman with bold makeup, vivid background colors, stylized lighting, high saturation`

---

## X Fantasy

Rich fantasy illustration with detailed environments, dramatic color palettes, and an immersive aesthetic.

**Pick this when:**
- You're generating fantasy settings or characters
- Detailed environments and atmospheric depth matter
- You want an illustrated look with painterly quality

**Avoid when:** you want clean modern realism — use X Sweet or Photorealism instead.

**Example prompt:** `fantasy warrior woman, armored outfit, enchanted forest background, dramatic lighting, rich colors, detailed illustration`

---

## X Dream

Dark, moody aesthetic with muted tones, atmospheric lighting, and an intimate, surreal feel.

**Pick this when:**
- You want a dark, atmospheric, or introspective look
- Muted color palettes and soft shadows are the aesthetic
- You want something dreamlike and ambient

**Avoid when:** you want bright, vivid output — use X Vision or X Fantasy instead.

**Example prompt:** `a woman in a dimly lit room, soft candlelight, muted tones, dark atmosphere, intimate portrait, dreamy`

---

## X Pixel

Anime-adjacent with neon colors, screen-lit aesthetics, and a digital illustration feel.

**Pick this when:**
- You want a cyberpunk or digital anime look
- Neon lighting and screen-lit color palettes are the goal
- You want anime style with an urban, tech-forward aesthetic

**Avoid when:** you want traditional anime style — use Anime instead.

**Example prompt:** `cyberpunk anime girl, neon lights, dark city background, glowing screen light, teal and pink palette, digital illustration`

---

## X Flat

Clean flat illustration with minimal shading, graphic shapes, and a modern design aesthetic.

**Pick this when:**
- You want a minimal, graphic illustration style
- Clean lines and flat color fills are the aesthetic
- You want something closer to graphic design than painting

**Avoid when:** you want detailed linework or shading — use Anime or Artoon instead.

**Example prompt:** `flat illustration, woman in a modern outfit, minimal shading, clean lines, pastel palette, graphic style`

---

## X Wonder

Chibi-influenced cartoon with warm tones, exaggerated proportions, and a playful, expressive style.

**Pick this when:**
- You want a cute, chibi, or super-deformed aesthetic
- Exaggerated proportions and expressive faces are the goal
- A warm, playful color palette is part of the look

**Avoid when:** you want realistic proportions — use any realism model instead.

**Example prompt:** `chibi girl, oversized head, big expressive eyes, warm orange tones, playful pose, cute cartoon style`

---

## Movie

Cinematic color grading with a film production look — rich tones, cinematic framing, and high-production detail.

**Pick this when:**
- You want the result to look like a still from a film
- Color grading, film grain, and cinematic framing matter
- You want a polished, high-budget production aesthetic

**Avoid when:** you want clean natural photography — use Photorealism instead.

**Example prompt:** `film still, woman in a noir setting, cinematic color grade, dramatic shadows, high production quality, movie aesthetic`
