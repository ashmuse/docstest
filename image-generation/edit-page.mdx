The Edit page is PornX's post-generation workspace.

<Card title="Open the Edit page" icon="pen-tool" horizontal href="https://pornx.co/edit">
  Pick an image and start editing on pornx.co.
</Card>

Select any image from your creations or collections, choose an editing tool, and refine the result without regenerating from scratch.

---

## Choosing an Image

Click **Choose image** on the Edit page to open the image picker. You can select from two sources:

- **Creations** — images you've generated, organized by date
- **Collections** — images you've saved or liked

Select any image to open it in the editor.

---

## Canvas Controls

Once an image is open, you can navigate the canvas freely:

- **Move** — drag the image to reposition it on the canvas
- **Zoom** — zoom in or out directly in the browser
- **Undo / Redo** — step back or forward through your edits, similar to Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y

---

## Tools

Select your tool from the menu at the bottom of the editor.

### Inpaint

Draw a mask over the area you want to change, enter a prompt describing what should replace it, and the AI repaints only that region. Everything outside the mask stays intact.

**Mask controls:**
- **Draw** — paint the area to edit
- **Erase** — remove parts of the mask
- **Undo / Redo** — adjust the mask before running the edit

<Tip>
  Use a tight mask around the specific area you want to change. A smaller, more precise mask produces cleaner results.
</Tip>

---

### Upscale

Enhances the image to 4× resolution. Use it to increase output size for downloading or publishing.

---

### Reimagine

<Info>
  Reimagine costs tokens, and Premium accounts pay less. It can be temporarily open to free accounts.
</Info>

Generates a new variation of the full image based on a text prompt. Use it to shift the overall look, mood, or composition while keeping the general structure of the original.

---

### Upscale Pro

<Info>
  Upscale Pro costs tokens, and Premium accounts pay less. It can be temporarily open to free accounts.
</Info>

Highest-fidelity upscale. In addition to increasing resolution, Upscale Pro enhances fine detail — skin texture, hair strands, fabric, lighting — at full output size.

| | Upscale | Upscale Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Resolution | 4× | 4× |
| Detail enhancement | — | ✓ |
| Cost | Tokens | Tokens, lower for Premium |

<Tip>
  Use Upscale Pro as the final step after finishing edits with Inpaint or Reimagine.
</Tip>
