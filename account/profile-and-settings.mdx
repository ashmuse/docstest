## Profile

In your [account settings](https://pornx.co/account/settings) you can set:

- A custom **display name**, shown on your profile.
- An **avatar**, chosen from your own creations on PornX. Uploading files or linking to images from other services is not supported.
- Links to your socials, including X and Discord.

Your public profile shows your published creations.

## Language

PornX is available in English, Spanish, German, French, Japanese, Russian and Portuguese. Change the language with the language switcher in the page footer. Some texts may still appear in English if a translation is missing. These docs are in English only.
