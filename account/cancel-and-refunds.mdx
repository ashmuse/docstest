## Cancel your subscription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your account">
    Log in and open your [account](https://pornx.co/account/subscription).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open Subscription">
    Go to the **Subscription** tab.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Cancel">
    Click **Cancel** and confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You keep all Premium features until the end of the current billing period. Subscriptions paid in crypto run for a fixed period and do not renew automatically.

## Refunds

Refund rules are on the [Refund policy](https://pornx.co/refund) page. In short:

- Refunds are reviewed case by case.
- You must cancel the subscription first.
- A refund is possible only if you have not made significant use of the AI service.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method.
- Tokens are not refundable.

To ask for a refund, contact [support](mailto:support@pornx.co) with your transaction ID.

<Note>
  The Refund policy page is the binding text. If it differs from this summary, follow the policy page.
</Note>
