You can pay for Premium and for tokens from [/premium](/premium).

| Method | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Credit and debit cards | Visa, Mastercard, American Express and other major cards. |
| Apple Pay and Google Pay | Available at checkout. |
| Cryptocurrency | Subscriptions paid in crypto run for a fixed period and do not renew automatically. See [Pay with crypto](/account/crypto-payments). |
| Pay by Bank | For token purchases. Shown only when it is available for your country. |

## What appears on your statement

The charge appears as **CUR*Epiclens Technolog**. The name does not mention adult content.
