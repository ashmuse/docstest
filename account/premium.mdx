Premium unlocks the full platform. See [/premium](https://pornx.co/premium) for current pricing, billing periods and offers. Prices are not listed in these docs because they change.

## Explore tier vs Premium

| Feature | Explore (free) | Premium |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Image generations per day | 4 | Unlimited |
| Image models, actions and styles | Free selection | All, including unique models |
| Image quality | Base | High and Best |
| Image aspect ratios | Limited selection | Full selection |
| Private generation | — | ✓ |
| Monthly token allowance | — | ✓ |
| Character creation | Costs tokens | 5 free per month |
| Inpaint and Upscale | Cost tokens | Included |
| Video quality | 480p | Higher resolutions |
| Chat messages | Daily limit | Unlimited |
| Chat model | Flash | Pro |
| Advanced chat memory | — | ✓ |
| Chatbot creation | See [Create a chatbot](/chat/create-a-chatbot) | Free |
| Gallery access | Limited | Full access and search filters |
| Discount on token packs | — | ✓ |

<Note>
  Some Edit tools, such as Reimagine and Upscale Pro, can be temporarily open to free accounts. Premium accounts always pay less for them.
</Note>

## How to upgrade

Go to [/premium](https://pornx.co/premium) and select a plan. See [Payment methods](/account/payments) for what is accepted.

Premium activates immediately after payment.

## Tokens and Premium

Premium includes a monthly token allowance, and unused tokens roll over. Some actions still cost tokens, such as video generation. Tokens are purchased separately and don't expire. See [Tokens](/account/tokens).

## Unlimited video access

Premium includes an unlimited video access option. Its exact terms are shown on [/premium](https://pornx.co/premium) under **Unlimited Video Access Terms**.

## Cancellation

You can cancel at any time. Open your account, go to **Subscription**, click **Cancel** and confirm. You keep Premium features until the end of your current billing period. See [Cancel and refunds](/account/cancel-and-refunds).
