The referral program rewards you with tokens when people you invite use PornX.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Share your link">
    Open [pornx.co/referrals](https://pornx.co/referrals) and copy your personal link. When someone signs up with it, you both get bonus tokens.
  </Step>

  <Step title="They spend">
    When your referrals buy tokens or subscribe to a plan, their purchases are tracked automatically.
  </Step>

  <Step title="You earn">
    You earn bonus tokens from those purchases. Your commission rate rises as you earn more from referrals. Current rates are shown on the referrals page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your referral stats

The referrals page shows joined users, tokens earned this week, total earnings, and a list of everyone who joined with your link.

<Note>
  The referral program is for tokens. If you want to promote PornX as a business, see [Affiliate program](/earn/affiliates).
</Note>
