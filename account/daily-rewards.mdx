You can collect free tokens once a day on [pornx.co/get-tokens](https://pornx.co/get-tokens).

1. Log in.
2. Open the page and click **Collect Now**.

The reward grows if you log in 7 days in a row. Each day of the week shows its reward on the page.

Tokens roll over and do not expire while your account is active. See [Tokens](/account/tokens).
