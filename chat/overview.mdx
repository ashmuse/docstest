Chat lets you talk to AI characters in uncensored roleplay. Open [pornx.co/chat](https://pornx.co/chat) to start.

<Info>
  Chat is in public beta. Features and limits change as it evolves. Share feedback on [Discord](https://discord.gg/pornx-co).
</Info>

## What you can do

- Browse the character catalog, search it, filter by tag, or sort by **Trending**.
- Talk to any character. See [Talking to characters](/chat/talking-to-characters).
- Save characters to **Favorites** for quick access.
- Build your own chatbot and publish it to the public library. See [Create a chatbot](/chat/create-a-chatbot).
- Manage your characters and conversations. See [My characters and chats](/chat/my-characters-and-chats).
- Track how your chatbots perform. See [Creator Studio](/chat/creator-studio).

## Free and Premium

Free accounts have a daily message limit and use the Flash model. Premium accounts get unlimited chat messages, the Pro model, advanced chat memory, and free chatbot creation. See [Premium plans](/account/premium).

## Rules

All characters are fictional AI companions for entertainment. Chat is not therapy or professional advice. Content involving anyone under 18 is never allowed, including in roleplay. Chat is moderated with automated checks and manual review. See the [Community guidelines](/help-section/community-guidelines).
