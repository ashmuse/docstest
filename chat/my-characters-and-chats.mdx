Chat keeps three personal lists in one place.

| List | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **My Characters** | The chatbots you created. Open one to edit it. |
| **My Chats** | Your conversations, so you can continue where you stopped. |
| **Favorites** | Characters you saved from the catalog for one-click access. |

To add a character to Favorites, use the favorite button on its card or page.

To change who can see a chatbot, edit it and change its visibility. See [Create a chatbot](/chat/create-a-chatbot).
