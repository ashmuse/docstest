Open a character from the [Chat catalog](https://pornx.co/chat) and send a message. Replies stream in as they are written. When many people are chatting, you may see your place in a queue.

## Models

| Model | Available to |
| --- | --- |
| Flash | All accounts |
| Pro | Premium |

Choose the model in the chat window. Premium accounts also get advanced chat memory.

## Message limit

Free accounts have a daily message limit that resets on a rolling 24-hour basis. When you reach it, you can upgrade to Premium for unlimited messages, or buy a message pack with tokens. After you buy a pack, the message that was blocked is sent automatically. See [/premium](https://pornx.co/premium) for rates.

## Tools in a conversation

- **Regenerate** a reply you do not like.
- **Edit** a message.
- **Personas** let you choose who you are in the roleplay. Free accounts can keep a limited number of personas.
- **Photo requests** ask the character for a picture. This costs tokens and needs a character linked to your image generator.
- **Media gallery** collects the images from a conversation.
- **Share** a conversation.
- **Report** or **block** a character or content that breaks the rules.

## Safety

Some messages trigger safety checks. If a message is blocked, rewrite it. Read the [Community guidelines](/help-section/community-guidelines) for what is not allowed.
