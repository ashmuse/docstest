You can build your own character and let others talk to it. Chatbot creation is free for Premium accounts. You need to be logged in.

Go to [pornx.co/chat](https://pornx.co/chat) and click **Create**.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name and tagline">
    Give the character a name and a short tagline that appears in the catalog.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Personality and scenario">
    Describe who the character is and the situation where the chat starts.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Greeting and example dialogs">
    Write the first message and add example dialogs that show how the character speaks.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Private definition">
    Add instructions that shape the character but are hidden from people who chat with it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Tags and avatar">
    Pick tags so people can find the character. Choose an avatar from your own creations or upload one.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Visibility and rating">
    Choose **Public**, **Unlisted** or **Private**. Set the content rating to **SFW**, **NSFW** or **Explicit**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The character card has a size limit. If the editor reports that the card is too long, shorten the personality, scenario or example dialogs.

## Review of public bots

Before publishing, a character goes through a moderation check. Public characters are also reviewed before they appear in the catalog. Keep to the [Community guidelines](/help-section/community-guidelines). See also [Content issues](/troubleshooting/content).
