Creator Studio shows how people use the chatbots you published. It is available at [pornx.co/chat/analytics](https://pornx.co/chat/analytics).

It reports:

- Replies
- Chatters
- Chat depth
- Approval
- Reroll rate
- Automatic insights about your characters

Creator Studio is for analytics only. Chat does not pay creators directly. To earn tokens from your images, see the [Creator Program](/earn/creator-program).
