The Creator Program lets creators showcase their work in the community feed, earn tokens when other users unlock their prompts, and try new features early.

## Requirements

To apply you need to:

- Have used PornX for at least 7 days.
- Have generated more than 1,000 images.
- Have a verified email.
- Be logged in.

## How to join

Open [pornx.co/creator-program](https://pornx.co/creator-program) and click **Apply now**. The team reviews your account within 7 days.

## Rules

- Your public content must follow the [Community guidelines](/help-section/community-guidelines).
- Most of your generated images should be visible in your profile.

<Note>
  Content you generate with [Private generation](/image-generation/private-generation) stays hidden from the Gallery unless you publish it through the program.
</Note>
