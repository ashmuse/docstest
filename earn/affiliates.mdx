The affiliate program is for people who promote PornX to adult audiences, such as site owners, social media creators, traffic buyers and channel admins. It is separate from the [Referral program](/account/referrals) for regular users.

See [pornx.co/affiliates](https://pornx.co/affiliates) for current terms.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Apply">
    Apply on the affiliates page. Approval usually takes less than 24 hours.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Promote">
    Use banners, deep links or promo text on your site or channels.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Earn">
    You earn a revenue share from paid subscriptions and token purchases of the users you bring. Tracking is in real time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Payouts

Payouts are weekly, by bank transfer or USDT.

## Rules

Affiliates must follow these rules:

- 18+ audiences only.
- No spam or misleading ads.
- No child, non-consensual or illegal content.
- No impersonation or stolen likenesses.
