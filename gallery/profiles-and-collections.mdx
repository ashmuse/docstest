Every user has a profile page. Your own profile is where you find your creations and saved items.

## Profile tabs

| Tab | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| Creations | Your images and videos, split into **Images** and **Videos**. |
| Collections | Items you saved, organized in folders. |
| About | Profile information and links to socials such as Discord. |

To change your display name, avatar and socials, see [Profile and settings](/account/profile-and-settings).

## Collections and folders

Saved items go into your collections. Items you like are kept in the **Likes** folder. You can organize saved items in your own folders:

- **Create folder** to add a new folder.
- **Rename folder** or **Delete folder** to manage existing ones.
- **Select images** to pick several items at once.

You can pick images from your creations and collections in the [Edit page](/image-generation/edit-page).
