These features are part of the video generator at [pornx.co/video](https://pornx.co/video).

## Actions

Actions are ready-made motions you can pick instead of describing them. There are separate action libraries for [Image-to-Video](/video-generation/image-to-video) and [Text-to-Video](/video-generation/text-to-video). New actions are added over time. See the [changelog](https://pornx.co/changelog).

## Two actions in one video

You can apply two actions in a single video. This feature is in beta.

## Smooth mode

Smooth mode makes motion in the finished clip smoother. While it runs, the video shows the status **Smoothing video**.

## Live preview

You see a preview while the video is being generated.

## Sessions

Videos are grouped into sessions. See [Sessions](/video-generation/sessions).

## Unlimited video access

Premium includes an unlimited video access option. Its terms are listed on [/premium](/premium) under **Unlimited Video Access Terms**. Check them there, because they can change.
