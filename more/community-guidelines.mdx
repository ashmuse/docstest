## Universal rules: zero tolerance

These rules apply to every product and every surface: image and video generation, character cards, avatars, greetings, chat messages, and anything else on PornX. Violations in this section result in immediate, permanent account termination.

**Minors.** Any content involving characters who could be interpreted as under 18 is strictly prohibited, real or fictional, sexual or not. This includes:

- Characters with childlike appearance, body, or behavior, regardless of stated age. "She's actually a 1000-year-old vampire" does not work here.
- "Aging up" characters who are canonically minors in their source material, or characters from media aimed at children.
- School, daycare, and other settings centered on minors.
- Grooming patterns: waiting for someone to turn 18, "I've watched you grow up," mentor or guardian relationships with a pre-adult history.

If a character's age is not obvious, state it explicitly in the character's Personality. This rule is enforced automatically and manually. Confirmed child sexual abuse material is removed, preserved as required by law, and reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant law enforcement authorities, together with associated account information.

**Real people.** All characters must be AI-generated and fictional. Content that resembles, mimics, or could be identified as a real living person is prohibited: public figures and private individuals alike. No real photographs, no deepfakes, no composites, no "inspired by" characters with a real person's name or likeness.

**Non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII).** Zero tolerance for creating, sharing, or threatening to share intimate content depicting a real person without their consent. This includes so-called revenge pornography, sextortion, "nudified" or undressed versions of real photographs, and covertly captured intimate images. If you believe intimate content depicting you appears on PornX, contact [support@pornx.co](mailto:support@pornx.co); verified removal requests are prioritized and processed without delay.

**Bestiality and zoophilia.** No sexual content involving animals or creatures with animal-level intelligence, real or fictional. Anthropomorphic characters are allowed in Chat only if they have full human-level intelligence, speech, and the ability to consent. "Animal instincts" or "heat cycles" are not an excuse to remove consent.

**Coprophilia.** Not allowed.

**Sex trafficking and prostitution.** Zero tolerance for any use of the platform connected to real-world prostitution, escorting, or trafficking. Suspected violations may be reported to law enforcement.

**Illegal content.** Do not generate or share content that violates applicable laws or regulations.

**Harassment and hate speech.** Zero tolerance for harassment, bullying, or hate speech based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or any other characteristic. This includes characters built to promote discrimination or violent ideologies.

**Doxxing and private information.** Do not share another person's private information anywhere on PornX: photographs, home or work address, phone number, email, payment details, account credentials, or any other identifying data shared without that person's consent.

**Impersonation and fraud.** Do not impersonate other users or PornX staff, misrepresent your identity or affiliation, or engage in fraudulent activity.

**Service abuse and moderation bypass.** Attempts to access paid features without payment, or to overload the platform in a way that degrades service for other users, will result in immediate restriction without refund. The same applies to attempts to circumvent, disable, or "jailbreak" our safety filters and moderation systems, including deliberately misspelled or coded prompts, prompt injection, and any other technique designed to produce content these guidelines prohibit. Attempting to generate prohibited content is a violation even if the attempt fails.

## Image and video generation

These rules apply to all visual content: generations, shared gallery content, and character avatars in Chat.

**Non-consensual scenarios.** Visuals portraying non-consent, or characters who are asleep, unconscious, intoxicated, or otherwise unable to consent, are not allowed.

**Violence.** Visuals depicting violence, physical harm, coercion, or signs of harm are prohibited. This includes handcuffs used in a coercive context, bruising, and scarring.

**Watermarks and AI provenance.** Generations include a PornX watermark identifying the content as AI-generated. Do not remove, modify, or obscure it, and do not strip metadata or other provenance signals. Do not edit generated content without explicit written permission from PornX. Depending on your jurisdiction, the law may require you to label AI-generated content as such when you share it elsewhere; that responsibility is yours.

Chat is text roleplay between fictional, consenting adults. Written fiction allows darker themes than visual content, but the universal rules above apply in full, and the rules below draw the line.

### Never use

These are prohibited in character cards and in chats, with no exceptions:

**Non-consent as the scenario.** No rape or sexual assault roleplay. No greetings that start with the user's character already restrained, assaulted, drugged, or unable to respond. No drugging, hypnosis, or mind control that removes the ability to refuse. No blackmail or captivity where refusing is not a real option.

**Blood-related incest.** No sexual or romantic content between blood relatives: parents and children, siblings including half-siblings, grandparents, aunts and uncles, cousins. Direct or implied, no exceptions through vague wording.

**Real-world violence.** No recreations of school shootings, terror attacks, war crimes, or genocide. No glorification of real criminals or violent extremists. No graphic gore: detailed torture, dismemberment, mutilation, or sexualized death.

**Self-harm.** No instructions for or encouragement of self-harm, suicide, or eating disorders. Not as a character trait, not as a scenario.

### Restricted use: allowed with a consent framework

Dark themes are part of adult fiction. They are allowed when the fiction is built correctly:

**CNC and kink.** Consensual non-consent, dominance, and rough play are allowed when all characters are clearly adults, consent is established inside the fiction, and any character can refuse or withdraw at any time. A captive who begs to be released and is ignored is a violation. A partner who agreed to a scene and can stop it is fiction.

**Dark themes.** Cheating, NTR, corruption, mafia and crime settings, sadistic and unhinged personalities are allowed as fiction. Characters must keep their agency, and scenarios must not mirror real-world exploitation: no therapist exploiting a patient, no boss trading employment for sex, no scenario where survival depends on compliance.

**Power dynamics.** Boss and employee, royalty and commoner, and similar imbalances are allowed when both characters have genuine choice and refusing carries no threat.

**Step and in-law relationships.** Allowed only when all characters are explicitly stated to be adults and explicitly not blood-related. Blood incest remains banned regardless of framing.

**Fictional violence.** Combat, action, and supernatural conflict are allowed in a clear fictional context. No graphic gore, and never combined with sexual non-consent.

### Character cards

Everything in a character card counts: Name, Tagline, Greeting, Personality, Description, and Avatar.

- **Avatars** follow the image and video generation rules above. AI-generated only, no real people.
- **Age** must be stated in the Personality if it is not obvious from the card.
- **Greetings** must not put the user's character in a situation they cannot consent to or exit.
- **Content Rating and tags** must be honest. NSFW content must be rated NSFW. Kink and dark themes must be tagged so users know what they are opening.
- **Public characters** are listed in the catalog and are subject to moderation review. Private characters are not listed, but every rule on this page still applies to them.
- **Originality and intellectual property.** Public characters must be your own creation. Do not copy other creators' characters, here or from other platforms, and do not upload content that infringes someone else's copyright, trademark, or other rights. Rights holders can submit takedown requests to [support@pornx.co](mailto:support@pornx.co).
- **No advertising.** No external links, promotions, solicitation, spam, or unsolicited commercial content in character cards or chats.

Characters that violate these guidelines will be removed without notice.

## How moderation works

We use a combination of automated systems and human review to enforce these guidelines across every surface of PornX: prompts, generations, character cards, avatars, and chat messages. Content flagged by our systems or reported by users may be reviewed by our trust and safety team, and we may review interactions as part of quality assurance. Private content is not exempt: private characters and private generations are subject to the same rules and the same enforcement.

## Reporting violations

If you see content that violates these guidelines, report it: use **Report message** in any chat, report a character from its card, or report gallery content. We review all reports and take action where guidelines are breached. You can also reach us at [support@pornx.co](mailto:support@pornx.co).

If you believe intimate content depicting you, or content infringing your rights, appears on PornX, email [support@pornx.co](mailto:support@pornx.co) with a link to the content and a short description. Complaints of this kind are reviewed and resolved promptly, and we will tell you the outcome.

## Consequences

Depending on severity, violations result in content removal, a warning, temporary restriction, or permanent account termination. Zero-tolerance violations skip straight to permanent termination. Where required or permitted by law, we report violations to the relevant authorities, including NCMEC and law enforcement.

We may also take action based on serious off-platform conduct that directly affects the safety of our community, in particular anything involving minors.

PornX enforces these guidelines at its sole discretion.

## Appeals

If you believe your content was removed or your account was restricted in error, you can appeal by emailing [support@pornx.co](mailto:support@pornx.co) within 30 days of the action. Include the affected content or character and why you believe the decision was wrong. Appeals are reviewed by a human, and we will respond with the outcome. Zero-tolerance violations involving minors, real people, or NCII are not eligible for appeal.
