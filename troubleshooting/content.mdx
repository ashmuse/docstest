PornX moderates content with automated checks and manual review. This page covers the most common cases where a generation or a published item does not appear as expected.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My generation was blocked">
    Some prompts fall outside the rules of the platform and are refused. Rewrite the prompt and try again. Read the [Community guidelines](/help-section/community-guidelines) to see what is not allowed. Content involving anyone under 18, real people without verifiable consent, or impersonation is never allowed, including in text prompts and chat roleplay.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My chatbot was not published">
    Public chatbots go through a moderation check and review before they appear in the Chat catalog. Until then, the bot stays available to you. Edit the character to remove anything that breaks the [Community guidelines](/help-section/community-guidelines). See [Create a chatbot](/chat/create-a-chatbot).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My image or video disappeared from the Gallery">
    Published content can be removed if it breaks the [Community guidelines](/help-section/community-guidelines) or after a content removal request. If you think it was removed by mistake, contact [support](mailto:support@pornx.co) with the approximate time of the generation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I want content removed">
    Use the [content removal form](https://pornx.co/content-removal). Requests are normally resolved within 7 business days.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
